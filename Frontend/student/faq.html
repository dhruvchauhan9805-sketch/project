Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is CampusBuzz?
CampusBuzz is a college event management system that allows students to view events, book tickets, check results, and interact with teachers and administrators online.
2. How do I register on CampusBuzz?
Go to the Login/Register page, select your role (Student/Teacher), enter your details, and create your account using your college email ID.
3. I forgot my password. What should I do?
Click on the “Forgot Password” option on the login page. Enter your registered email ID to reset your password.
4. Who can create or update events?
Only teachers and administrators have permission to create, update, or delete events.
5. How can I check event results?
Students can visit the “Results” page to view event results uploaded by teachers.
